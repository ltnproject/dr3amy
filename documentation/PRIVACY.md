# Privacy
- No accounts, analytics, telemetry or third-party requests. No fonts or scripts are loaded from other origins.
- Data is stored in the browser's IndexedDB for this site's origin.
- **Browser storage is not permanent.** Clearing site data, private windows, uninstalling the browser or storage pressure can delete it. "Request persistent storage" in Settings helps but is not a guarantee. Different browsers and devices do not sync.
- Back up from Settings and keep the `.dr3amy` file somewhere safe.
- Data leaves the device only when you export or share a file.
